Privacy

A simple privacy baseline.

This site is designed as a personal professional portfolio and does not need invasive tracking to be useful.

The current static project does not include analytics, advertising trackers, account creation, or a server-side contact submission service.

The contact form performs validation in the browser. If a verified email address is configured, it can prepare a message in the visitor’s email application; it does not silently transmit the form to a server.

If analytics, a contact backend, cookies, or other third-party services are introduced later, this page should be updated to describe those services accurately before deployment.
